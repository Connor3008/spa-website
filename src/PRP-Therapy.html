How It Works

PRP Therapy begins with a small sample of your blood, which is then processed to isolate the platelet-rich plasma. This concentrated PRP is injected into targeted areas of the skin or scalp to stimulate cellular repair, collagen production, and hair regrowth. The procedure is quick, safe, and highly effective.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is PRP Therapy safe?

Yes, PRP Therapy is safe and uses your body’s natural platelets, reducing the risk of allergic reactions or complications.

How many sessions will I need?

Most clients see optimal results after 3–4 sessions, spaced about 4–6 weeks apart, depending on their goals.

When will I see results?

Results vary, but most clients begin noticing improvements in skin texture or hair growth within a few weeks of treatment.

Our Mission

At Impressions Med Spa & Laser, our mission is to provide cutting-edge aesthetic treatments with a personalized touch, helping our clients achieve their beauty goals in a supportive and welcoming environment. We strive to enhance confidence and well-being through innovative procedures and exceptional care.

Meet Our Team

Dr. David C. Best

Medical Director

Dr. David C. Best, M.D., is one of the country’s most prominent and well-respected plastic and reconstructive surgeons. He established a successful private practice in Greensboro, North Carolina, and is board-certified by the American Board of Plastic Surgery.

Dr. Best's mission is to provide his patients with unique, personalized care, helping them achieve their best impression. With numerous accolades and media features, including in Cosmopolitan, Elle, and Vogue, Dr. Best brings unparalleled expertise and passion to every patient interaction.

Lynn Dailey Hege

Medical Aesthetician & Laser Technician

Lynn Hege is a highly experienced Medical Aesthetician and Laser Technician with two decades of expertise. She is skilled in a variety of advanced laser technologies and aesthetic procedures, bringing a unique blend of technical skill and artistic vision.

Lynn is committed to individualized care and works closely with patients to address their unique skin concerns, helping them achieve their goals through state-of-the-art techniques and compassionate attention.

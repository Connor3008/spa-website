How It Works

IPL Therapy uses broad-spectrum light to target pigmentation and vascular irregularities in the skin. The light energy penetrates the skin, breaking down unwanted pigmentation and stimulating the body's natural healing process. Over time, the skin appears clearer, smoother, and more youthful.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is IPL Therapy painful?

Most clients experience minimal discomfort, often described as a mild warming sensation during the procedure.

How many sessions are needed?

While results vary, most clients achieve optimal results with 3–5 sessions, spaced about 4 weeks apart.

Is there any downtime?

IPL Therapy requires little to no downtime, allowing you to resume daily activities immediately after treatment.

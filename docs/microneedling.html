How It Works

RF Microneedling uses ultra-fine needles to create controlled micro-injuries in the skin, triggering the body’s natural healing process. At the same time, radiofrequency energy is delivered deep into the skin layers to stimulate collagen and elastin production. This dual-action approach enhances skin texture, tone, and firmness.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is RF Microneedling safe?

Yes, RF Microneedling is a safe and effective treatment when performed by trained professionals. It is suitable for most skin types.

How many treatments will I need?

While some clients notice results after one session, a series of 3–5 treatments is often recommended for optimal results.

What is the recovery time?

Recovery time is minimal, with most clients experiencing mild redness for 1–2 days post-treatment.
